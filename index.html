Guidelines

House Rules & Policies

To ensure a peaceful, safe, and pleasant living environment for all residents, Sun Haven Hostel follows these standard guidelines:

1

Entry & Curfew Timings

Main gates close at 10:30 PM daily for safety. Late entries must be pre-informed to the warden with valid reason.

2

Visitor Policy

Parents and guardians are allowed in the common lobby area. Unregistered overnight guests are strictly prohibited.

3

No Smoking & Alcohol

Sun Haven is a strictly non-smoking and alcohol-free zone. Violation leads to immediate termination of hostel agreement without refund.

4

Rent Payment Due Date

Monthly rent must be paid on or before the 5th of every calendar month to avoid late fee penalties.
